Read before you start

  • Use Google Chrome or Edge on a laptop/PC. Phones and tablets are not supported.
  • Close every other app, browser tab and window. Turn off notifications and screen savers. Plug in your charger.
  • Sit in a well-lit room, face the camera, and stay alone. Keep your face visible for the whole exam.
  • Click Allow for camera and full screen when asked. The exam will not start without both.
  • Do not switch tabs or windows, press Esc, minimise the browser, or unplug the camera.
  • You get one warning. The next violation ends your exam immediately and it cannot be restarted.
  • Do not refresh or close the page. If something breaks, tell your teacher; do not retry on your own.
  • Copy, paste and right-click are disabled. Webcam photos and activity are recorded for review.
