# Online exam: instructions for students

Read this fully the day before the exam. Most problems come from skipping these steps.

## Before the exam (do this 1 day earlier)
1. Use a **laptop or desktop** with a working webcam. Phones and tablets will not work.
2. Use the latest **Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge**. Update it if needed.
3. Check your internet. Stay on one stable connection; do not switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.
4. Open the exam link and press **Start Exam** only on exam day. On the day before, just check that the page opens and that your browser asks for camera access.
5. Charge your laptop and keep the charger plugged in.
6. Know your **student ID** exactly as the teacher gave it.

## Just before the exam (10 minutes early)
1. **Close everything else:** other browser tabs and windows, WhatsApp/Telegram/Teams/Zoom, music, games, screen recorders, VPNs.
2. Turn off notifications (Windows: Focus Assist / Do Not Disturb) and disable screen savers and auto-lock.
3. Sit in a **well-lit room** with light on your face, not behind you. Be **alone**, with the door closed.
4. Clear your desk. Keep only what your teacher has allowed.
5. Put your phone on silent, **out of reach**.
6. Make sure nothing else is using your camera (close other video apps).

## Starting the exam
1. Open the exam link in Chrome/Edge.
2. Type your **student ID**, tick **"I have read and accept the exam rules"**, press **Start Exam**.
3. Click **Allow** when the browser asks for the **camera**, and allow **full screen**.
4. Make sure your face is visible in the small camera box on the right. Stay that way for the whole exam.

## During the exam: strictly follow
- **Do not** change tabs, open another window or app, minimise the browser, or press **Esc / Alt+Tab / Windows key**.
- **Do not** refresh, go back, or close the page.
- **Do not** click outside the browser window (for example on a second monitor). Disconnect extra monitors before you start.
- **Do not** unplug, cover, or turn off the camera.
- Copy, paste, and right-click are disabled. Do not try them.
- Do not talk to anyone or leave your seat.
- You have **one warning**. If you see a warning box, click **Return to exam** right away. A second violation ends the exam immediately and it **cannot be restarted**.
- Submit the Google Form with the **Submit** button at the end. Wait until you see the "response recorded" message.

## If something goes wrong
- **Camera or full-screen permission blocked:** click the lock/camera icon near the address bar, choose Allow, reload, and start again (this is allowed before the exam has started).
- **Internet drops:** reconnect quickly. Do not close the page. Tell your teacher right after the exam.
- **Power cut, crash, or accidental termination:** contact your teacher immediately. Do not keep retrying. Your teacher can check the log and reset your attempt.
- **Camera disconnects:** the exam ends automatically, so check the cable or camera before you start.

## Remember
Your webcam photos and activity (tab switches, full-screen exits) are recorded for your teacher to review.
